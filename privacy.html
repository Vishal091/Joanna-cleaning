Plain-language privacy notice

Your details, your choice.

This page explains what happens when you use the estimate request form or virtual assistant on Joana's Cleaning's website.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In brief: the estimate form prepares an email draft in your own email app. The virtual assistant answers preset questions locally in your browser. This static website does not transmit those chat messages to an AI service or store form submissions in a website database.

Information you may enter

The estimate form asks for your name, email address, ZIP code, requested service, and may optionally include a phone number, preferred cleaning frequency, and details about your space. The virtual assistant lets you type a question to match against its built-in set of common questions. Please do not enter sensitive or private information that is not needed for a cleaning estimate.

How the estimate form works

When you submit the form, your browser creates an email draft addressed to info@joanacleaning.com using your default email application. The site does not send the email on your behalf. You can review, edit, or discard the draft before sending it. If you send it, the business receives the information you chose to include and may use it to respond to your request and coordinate service.

How the virtual assistant works

The virtual assistant is a lightweight guided question-and-answer tool. Its answers are written in the website code and it uses simple keyword matching for typed questions. It does not connect to a generative AI model, send chat messages to an AI provider, or save chat history on the website.

Third-party resources

The site loads typography from Google Fonts and illustrative images from Unsplash, and links to email, telephone, Thumbtack and Google Maps. Loading external resources or opening an external service can cause your browser to communicate with those providers, which handle data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Cookies and analytics

The current static website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include an analytics script. Hosting providers and external resource providers may process technical request information to deliver their services. This should be reviewed again if analytics, online booking, payment processing, or other integrations are added.

Contact

For questions about a request you have sent, contact Joana's Cleaning at info@joanacleaning.com or (862) 290-8386.

Business review before launch

This is a short website notice, not legal advice. The business should review and approve it before launch so it accurately reflects the company's actual data practices and applicable legal obligations.
